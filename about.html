WHO WE ARE

Built on resources.
Growing into real assets.

HAR INFRA and HAR TRADING COMPANY together form an integrated business group that begins at the source — natural resource mining — and carries that value forward through material supply, real estate acquisition, infrastructure development and international trade.

Operating since 2011, the group has built a disciplined, resource-backed approach to growth: extract responsibly, supply reliably, and reinvest into long-term assets and market relationships that compound over time.

HAR INFRA PRIVATE LIMITED operates at the intersection of Natural Resources, Infrastructure & Real Estate. We are engaged in the mining, sourcing and supply of construction and mineral materials — sand, coal, crushed stone, aggregates, marble and other resources supporting infrastructure and construction projects.

Our vision extends beyond material supply. We are expanding into real estate investment, land acquisition, property development and project development, connecting resources, capital, land and execution to build long-term value.

FOUNDER & CEO · SINCE APRIL 2017

14+ years across mining, construction materials, real estate and project development.

Our business focuses on the sourcing, mining and supply of construction and mineral materials including sand, coal, crushed stone, aggregates, marble and other materials used across infrastructure and construction projects.

As Founder and CEO of HAR TRADING COMPANY, we lead the global export of natural resources — sand, coal, marble and aggregates — along with mining equipment and construction materials. Our mission is a diversified platform that bridges natural resources, infrastructure and real estate development, fostering partnerships with investors, developers, contractors and landowners to deliver innovative, commercially viable projects.
