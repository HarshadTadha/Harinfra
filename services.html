OUR BUSINESSES

Five businesses, one connected
operating model.

Each business line stands on its own and reinforces the others — resources create supply, supply supports development.

01

Mining & Natural Resources

Resource ownership and extraction across our operating network in Gujarat, Rajasthan and Maharashtra — the foundation of everything HAR builds on.

  • Aggregates & sand mining
  • Marble, granite & dimensional stone
  • Stone processing & handling
  • Responsible, long-term resource stewardship
02

Material Processing & Supply

Processing and coordinating construction materials for developers, contractors and infrastructure projects, with continuity built into every order.

  • Cement & refractory materials
  • Gypsum, blocks & construction inputs
  • Quality-controlled processing
  • Reliable, scheduled supply
03

Real Estate Acquisition & Development

Strategic acquisition and development of property opportunities, converting resource-side value into durable, long-term assets.

  • Site acquisition & feasibility
  • Project development & execution
  • Long-term asset building
  • Alignment with the group's material supply
04

Infrastructure & Construction

Delivering roads, civil works and construction execution, backed by the group's own material supply and resource network.

  • Roads & civil works
  • Construction project execution
  • Material-backed project delivery
  • Development-focused planning
05

Global Material Export

International supply of natural resources and construction materials, with logistics support across nine key export markets.

  • Gulf: Saudi Arabia, Oman, Qatar
  • South Asia: Bangladesh, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Maldives
  • East Africa: Kenya
  • Europe: United Kingdom
